# Review template — independent Codex

Verify the exact candidate against the original contract and raw evidence. Treat the
implementer's summary as claims to verify. State verified and unverified portions;
do not accept solely because the summary or checks claim success. Give concrete
rework, correction guidance and re-verification requirements without adding acceptance
criteria. Submit accepted, needs_changes or blocked separately through the bound tool.
Only independent review can accept work. Codinator checks format and bindings only.

## Subject
Identify original contract, delivery, attempt and exact candidate being reviewed.

## Verdict
State the independently determined result matching the submitted control field.

## Basis and verification
Relate findings to original acceptance criteria, actual artifacts, raw evidence and
required checks; distinguish verified facts, inferences and unverified portions.

## Rework
Give concrete problems, basis, correction guidance and re-verification needs or None.

## Stop reason
If blocked, identify who must provide what information, authority or resource; else None.
