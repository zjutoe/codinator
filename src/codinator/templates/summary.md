Report observed results and cite real artifacts and raw evidence. Use None, 无 or
未执行 honestly when appropriate; never invent facts or claim independent acceptance.

Submit the declared disposition separately through the bound tool. Codinator checks
format only; you are responsible for consistency between the declaration and content.

## Status
State the attempt/checkpoint identity and current declared disposition.

## Completed
List work actually completed, with its practical result.

## Incomplete
List outstanding requirements or None.

## Attempts and results
Describe actual attempts and observed outcomes; mark unperformed work explicitly.

## Artifacts and evidence
Locate real artifacts and raw evidence; identify candidate and check evidence.

## Deviations and unknowns
Identify deviations and unknowns or None; distinguish claims from verified facts.

## Next step
State the proposed next action, recipient and required input.
