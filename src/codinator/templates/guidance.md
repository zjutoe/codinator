# Guidance template — Codex guide

Answer the linked help request with actionable advice, its basis and unknowns.
Verify the declared candidate, branch and clean state before recommending automatic
continuation. Keep source and Git read-only. State boundaries for continuation,
renewed help or stopping, and submit continue or blocked explicitly through the bound
tool. Guidance cannot amend requirements, grant authority, extend budgets or accept
implementation; a fresh independent Codex later reviews the final candidate.
Codinator validates structure and identities, not whether your advice is sufficient.

## Question
Identify the linked request, attempt, original contract and specific question answered.

## Advice
Give actionable continuation advice within the original scope and authority.

## Basis
Explain supporting evidence, verified candidate state and relevant counterexamples.

## Unknowns
State unresolved facts and limits or None.

## Next boundary
State when to continue, ask again or stop, with the responsible handler and input.
